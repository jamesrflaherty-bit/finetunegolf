About Fine Tune Golf

Passion for the game. Precision in the craft. A shop where you always talk to the people doing the work.

Our story

Why Fine Tune Golf exists

Fine Tune Golf started from a simple belief: every golfer deserves equipment that actually fits.

Jeff Flaherty has been passionate about golf for more than 30 years. That passion became a mission to make sure every golfer in the Wadsworth area has clubs that fit their swing, not just whatever is on the shelf.

James brings his own expertise to the shop. He graduated from the Mitchell Golf 5-Day Master Class in 2024, and every loft and lie adjustment is set to exact specification on a Mitchell Golf machine.

Together, we run a shop focused on one thing: getting your clubs right. Whether it's a full custom fitting or a regrip, you'll always be talking to the people doing the work.

We love helping golfers. We do it for the pure enjoyment of seeing people improve, and for growing the game of golf.

How we work

Three things we hold ourselves to

Straightforward advice

We'll recommend what actually helps your game, even if your current clubs already fit you well.

Precision over speed

Every fitting, build and repair gets the time it needs to get the details right.

Local and personal

When you call or stop in, you're dealing directly with the people fitting, building and repairing your clubs.

Independent and unbiased

Our only goal is the right equipment for your swing.

We're not tied to a single brand or manufacturer. We recommend whatever suits your swing, which is sometimes the clubs you already have.
