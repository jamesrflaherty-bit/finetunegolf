Jeff & James

Our story

Why Fine Tune Golf exists

Fine Tune Golf started from a simple belief: every golfer deserves equipment that actually fits.

Jeff Flaherty has been passionate about golf for more than 30 years. That passion became a mission to make sure every golfer in Medina County has clubs that fit their swing, not just whatever is on the shelf.

James brings his own expertise to the shop. He graduated from the Mitchell Golf 5-Day Master Class in 2023, and every loft and lie adjustment is set to exact specification on a Mitchell Golf machine.

Together, we run a shop focused on one thing: getting your clubs right. Whether it's a full custom fitting or a regrip, you'll always be talking to the people doing the work.

We love helping golfers. We do it for the pure enjoyment of seeing people improve, and for growing the game of golf.

Fitting & club building

Jeff

Club builder for more than 30 years

Jeff has been building golf clubs for more than 30 years, mostly for the enjoyment of it and for helping his friends enjoy the game. He takes the time to understand what each golfer wants from their equipment, then helps them get clubs working with their swing rather than against it.

Masterclass graduate, 2023

James

Loft, lie and precision measurement

James graduated from the Mitchell Golf 5-Day Master Class in 2023, and every loft and lie adjustment in the shop is set to exact specification. He brings a precise, measurement-first approach to fittings, builds and repairs.

How we work

Three things we hold ourselves to

Straightforward advice

We'll recommend what actually helps your game, even if your current clubs already fit you well.

Precision over speed

Every fitting, build and repair gets the time it needs to get the details right.

Local and personal

When you call or stop in, you're dealing directly with the people fitting, building and repairing your clubs.

Independent and unbiased

Our only goal is the right equipment for your swing.

We're not tied to a single brand or manufacturer. We recommend whatever suits your swing, which is sometimes the clubs you already have.
