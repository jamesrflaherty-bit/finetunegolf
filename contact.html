Questions we hear most

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. We work by appointment, so please call or text to arrange a time. That includes club drop-offs, which we can arrange at a time that suits you.

Where are you?

We're at 130 Reimer Rd, Wadsworth, OH 44281. Please call or text before you visit so we can arrange a time. The directions button above shows the route.

How quickly will you reply?

Usually within one business day. If you need an answer sooner, call or text (330) 715-2282.

What are your hours?

We work by appointment, so there are no fixed opening hours. Call or text (330) 715-2282 and we'll find a time that works for you.

What's the difference between a fitting and a repair?

A fitting builds the right clubs for your swing, from the shaft and head up. A repair maintains or adjusts clubs you already own, such as a regrip, a reshaft or a loft and lie adjustment.
