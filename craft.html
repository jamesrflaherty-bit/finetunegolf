Frequency, not guesswork

Measured, not assumed.

When every club in a set progresses evenly, distances and gapping are easier to trust. We measure the two things that matter most.

Shaft frequency

The real flex, not the label.

Flex letters aren't standardized between brands, and two shafts with the same letter can play differently. Our Mitchell Golf DigiFlex 2.0 measures each shaft's actual frequency, so we can verify flex and match a set of parallel 0.370-inch tip shafts.

Our shaft frequency guide explains the numbers.

Loft & lie

Every degree, checked.

Loft and lie control launch, direction and how the sole meets the turf. Each club is measured on our Mitchell Golf Steelclub Signature Angle Machine, adjusted to spec, then checked again.

Single clubs or complete iron sets, so gapping stays consistent from one iron to the next.

Hand-built sets

From raw components to a finished set

Every build follows the same path, from components to your bag.

01

Select

We select KZG heads and shafts based on your fitting, your preferences and your budget.

02

Measure

Shafts are checked for frequency, and loft and lie are set and verified on the angle machine.

03

Match

Components are matched for consistency. Set shafts are frequency matched using parallel 0.370-inch tip shafts, so each club progresses from the one before it.

04

Build

Clubs are hand-assembled in our shop, then handed over ready to play.
