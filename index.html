How a fitting works

Every fitting starts with your swing.

We look at how you actually move, then match the shaft, head, loft and lie to it.

  1. 1

    Swing analysis

    We measure your swing speed, tempo and ball flight.

  2. 2

    Shaft selection

    We check shaft frequency, then match weight and profile to your swing.

  3. 3

    Head & loft fitting

    We pick a KZG head, then set loft, lie and grip size.

  4. 4

    Build & delivery

    Your clubs are hand-assembled in our shop, ready to play.
