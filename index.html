How a fitting works

Four steps from your swing to clubs you trust

Every fitting follows the same sequence, so each choice can be traced back to a measurement or a test.

1

Swing analysis

We evaluate your swing speed, tempo and ball flight tendencies to set a baseline for everything that follows.

2

Shaft selection

We check shaft frequency as a baseline, then recommend the right weight and profile for how you swing.

3

Head & loft fitting

We recommend a KZG head for your preferences and playing style, then set the proper loft, lie and grip size.

4

Build & delivery

Your clubs are carefully hand-assembled in our shop with KZG components, ready to play.
