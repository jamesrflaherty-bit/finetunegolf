What we do

Three services. One standard: precise work.

Whether you are building a new set or fixing the clubs you already own, every job gets the same careful attention.

Custom Club Fitting

Each fitting begins with measurement: swing speed, tempo and ball flight. Shaft, KZG head, loft, lie and grip size are then specified to the player.

How fitting works

Precision Club Building

Every club is assembled by hand in the workshop to a written specification. Set shafts are frequency matched on parallel 0.370-inch tips.

See our build process

Precision Calibrations

Reshafting, regripping, and loft and lie micro-adjustments for irons, woods and hybrids. Each change is measured before and after.

Repair services

How a fitting works

Four steps from your swing to clubs you trust. Every fitting follows the same sequence, so each choice can be traced back to a measurement or a test.

1

Swing analysis

We evaluate your swing speed, tempo and ball flight tendencies to set a baseline for everything that follows.

2

Shaft selection

We check shaft frequency as a baseline, then recommend the right weight and profile for how you swing.

3

Head & loft fitting

We recommend a KZG head for your preferences and playing style, then set the proper loft, lie and grip size.

4

Build & delivery

Your clubs are carefully hand-assembled in our shop with KZG components, ready to play.
