Loft & lie

When should I have my golf club loft and lie checked?

Check loft and lie when you buy used clubs, after a repair or hard bend, or whenever shots miss in one direction. They control launch, direction and how the sole meets the turf, and a few degrees can change how a set gaps.

We measure each club against its intended specification, adjust it, and check the result, on single clubs or complete iron sets.

What a check gives you

  • Each club checked individually
  • Consistent gapping across an iron set
  • Adjustments verified, not estimated
  • Straightforward advice on what's worth changing
