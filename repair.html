Loft & lie

The adjustments most golfers never check

Loft and lie control launch, direction and how the sole meets the turf. Measuring them takes minutes, and a few degrees can change how a set gaps and how it performs.

We measure each club against its intended specification, adjust it, and check the result, on single clubs or complete iron sets.

What a check gives you

  • Each club checked individually
  • Consistent gapping across an iron set
  • Adjustments verified, not estimated
  • Straightforward advice on what's worth changing

Repair FAQ

Questions we hear most

Do I need an appointment to drop off clubs?

Yes, please call ahead. Drop-offs are arranged by phone at a time that suits you, and they can be scheduled outside traditional business hours once they're coordinated with us.

Do you work on irons, woods and hybrids?

Yes. We reshaft and repair irons, woods and hybrids, and we can adjust single clubs or complete iron sets.

Can I get a shaft's frequency checked on its own?

Yes. Shaft frequency inspection is a service in its own right. Choose it on our contact form, or call or text us.

How much does a repair cost?

Labor prices for regrips, reshafts, loft and lie adjustments and other repairs are on our Services page. Prices shown are labor only. Grips, shafts, adapters and other components are additional. Club rebuilds are quoted per job. To arrange a drop-off, call or text (330) 715-2282.
