Common questions

What golf club repairs cost

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. All prices are labor; grips, shafts and other parts are extra.

How much does it cost to regrip golf clubs?

Standard regripping is $5 per club plus the grip, with precision alignment, custom tape build-up and a core sizing check included. If you bring your own grips, installation is $8 per club. Putter regrips are $10.

How much does it cost to reshaft a golf club?

Reshafting is $30 per club for steel and $35 per club for graphite, plus the shaft. Every reshaft includes spine alignment, and set reshafts are frequency matched using parallel 0.370-inch tip shafts.

How much is a loft and lie adjustment?

A single club is $7 for loft, lie or both. A full iron set of up to 8 clubs is $50 with a written before-and-after spec sheet, and extra irons or wedges are $6 each with a set. The $35 Precision Spec Blueprint, a full-bag loft, lie and length audit, is free with any adjustment.

How much does a shaft flex check cost?

A Shaft Flex Check is $35 for a full set, or $5 per club. We measure each shaft's frequency on a Mitchell Golf DigiFlex 2.0 and check loft, lie, length and swing weight across the set. It's free with any full-set reshaft.

Do you build custom golf clubs?

Yes. Custom club assembly is $20 per club, and an 8-club custom iron set is $150, frequency matched with parallel 0.370-inch tip shafts. As a KZG Authorized Fitter, we build with KZG components. See how we build.

How do I drop off my clubs?

All work is by appointment. Call or text (330) 715-2282 to arrange a drop-off time at our shop at 130 Reimer Rd in Wadsworth, Ohio.
